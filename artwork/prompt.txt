Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a single finished square app icon for Walk Window, a thoughtfully designed dog-walking weather app. No lettering.
Design a genuinely polished, distinctive illustrated app icon: an open cream-colored casement window reveals a person taking a dog for a relaxed walk on a curving sunlit path. The window and the dog walk must read as one cohesive symbol, not three unrelated clip-art pieces. Two gently angled open window sashes and a small sill make it unmistakably a real window. Inside, a graceful solid silhouette of a clothed person walking a small dog on a leash is prominent and clear, with a simple sweeping path receding into a warm outdoor view. The figures should occupy about half the height of the window opening, not tiny specks in a landscape.
Style: sophisticated contemporary illustration with precise, beautifully shaped forms and subtle layered-paper depth; clean soft edges and restrained shading. Thoughtful proportions, warm charm, confident negative space, immediately recognizable at home-screen size. Art directed like a premium independent app. Neither a crude road-sign pictogram nor a stock 3D emoji.
Palette fits the existing app: deep forest green background (#244d3e), warm ivory window, muted sage/teal landscape, warm amber sunlight/path. Limited harmonious colors. One small low sun is fine. Avoid a busy scenic painting.
Composition: centered, balanced, clear large silhouette; all essential artwork contained within a centered circle occupying 78% of the square's width so circular icon masks do not crop it. Opaque forest green fills the entire square edge to edge. No outer rounded app-tile border and no surrounding presentation mockup.
Constraints: no text, initials, logo words, watermarks, paw-print emblem, stick figure, squiggly stripe, dashboard/window UI chrome, excessive tiny detail, decorative badge, border outside the window, or extra objects. The result must look finished and purposeful, not like a simple geometric SVG assembled from basic shapes.
