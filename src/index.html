    How times are picked

    Each upcoming hourly point gets equal weight for precipitation, US AQI, UV, and temperature. Lower is better. Precipitation combines chance and amount. We compare percentile ranks across the forecast; ties go to the earliest hour.

    Before sunset covers sunrise to sunset. After sunset covers sunset to local midnight. Times use the forecast location’s time zone. Temperature and AQI are at the suggested start; precipitation and average UV cover the following hour. Forecasts do not guarantee conditions throughout a walk.

    No temperature bounds are applied yet: colder always ranks lower, even below freezing. These are relative comparisons, not a declaration that conditions are safe for your dog.

    Days without AQI are labeled weather-only and excluded from the week’s best combined picks. Missing readings are never treated as zero.

    Data & location

    Weather comes from Open-Meteo using the best available models at your coordinates. AQI comes from CAMS via Open-Meteo: a regional model, about 11 km in Europe and 45 km globally, usually covering only 4–5 days.

    Precise location improves the forecast lookup, but it does not turn modeled data into a backyard sensor. Location search uses a place’s center; coordinates or device location give a closer lookup.

    IQAir’s free API offers current city AQI. Individual stations and forecasts require paid plans, so IQAir is not used for these recommendations.

    Your last location and forecast are saved on this device. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve data. Free API use is noncommercial; data is under CC BY 4.0.

    Installation help

    On iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome or Edge, use Install app above or the browser’s install menu.

    The app can reopen with its last saved forecast offline. Fresh forecasts need an internet connection.
