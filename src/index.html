    How times are picked

    Within your walking hours and cutoffs, suggestions balance lower rain chance, cleaner air, lower UV, and cooler temperatures equally. Times are local to your selected location.

    Check conditions before heading out; each dog’s needs differ.

    Data & location

    Forecasts from Open-Meteo and CAMS are estimates; conditions on your walk may differ. Only days with complete forecasts are shown.

    Your chosen location is sent to Open-Meteo. Your location, preferences, and last forecast are saved on this device.

    Install Walk Window

    iPhone or iPad
    1. Open Walk Window in Safari.
    2. Tap Share (the square with an up arrow). You may need to tap the … menu first.
    3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If missing, find it under Edit Actions.
    4. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.
    Android
    1. Open Walk Window in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen or Install app.
    3. Choose Install if offered and follow the prompts.
    Computer

    In Chrome or Edge, look for Install in the address bar or browser menu. On a Mac in Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.
