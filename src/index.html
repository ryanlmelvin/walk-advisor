    How times are picked

    We skip hours outside your times and limits. Rain or snow, AQI, UV, and temperature get equal weight. Lower is better. Ties go to the earlier hour.

    Daylight picks fall between sunrise and sunset. Night picks run from sunset to midnight. All times are local to your chosen place.

    Temperature and AQI are for the start. Rain, snow, and UV cover the next hour. A weather-only pick has no AQI, so we cannot check its air limit. It cannot be a week’s best pick.

    These picks are a guide. They do not ensure a safe walk for every dog.

    Data & location

    Open-Meteo gives us weather data. CAMS gives us air data. Both use models, not a sensor in your yard. Air forecasts last about 4–5 days.

    GPS gives a closer lookup than a city name. Air data still covers a broad region.

    IQAir’s free plan gives current city data. Local station data and forecasts need a paid plan.

    We save your location, settings, and last forecast on this device. We send your chosen location to Open-Meteo for the forecast.
